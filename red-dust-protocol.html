⚠ You can leave a mission at any time, but it can’t be resumed. Progress is lost.
Your mission: keep a crew of 4 astronauts alive for 30 sols (Martian days).
Each sol, assign your crew to jobs. Every resource drains daily, and you can't cover everything at once.
Low power slows every system. Low morale slows your crew. A damaged hull leaks oxygen.
Random events force hard choices. If oxygen, water, food, power, morale or the hull hits zero, the mission fails.
